# Section 5307.4

## Canonical Code Text

5307.4 Carbon dioxide enrichment systems. The 
design, installation and maintenance of carbon 
dioxide enrichment systems with more than 45 kg 
of carbon dioxide, and carbon dioxide enrichment 
systems with any quantity of carbon dioxide having 
a remote fill connection, shall comply with Sections 
5307.4.1 through 5307.4.7. 
❖ 
This section was added due to the increasing 
number of facilities that cultivate plants 
indoors, and the potential hazard posed by 
intentionally introducing a possible asphyxiant 
into an occupiable space. Such provisions 
were needed, as the code had not addressed 
CO2, and this particular process needs specific 
requirements due to the hazard it creates. The 
provisions are similar to those for insulated 
liquid carbon dioxide used in beverage 
dispensing systems, as addressed in Section 
5307.3. The provisions apply at the quantity of 
45 kg, as does Section 5307.3. 
5307.4.1 
Documentation. The following 
information shall be provided with the application 
for permit: 
1. Total aggregate quantity of liquid carbon 
dioxide in kgs or cubic meter at normal 
temperature and pressure. 
2. Location and total volume of the room where 
the carbon dioxide enrichment operation will 
be conducted. Identify whether the room is at 
grade or below grade. 
3. Location of containers relative to equipment, 
building openings and means of egress. 
4. Manufacturer’s specifications and pressure 
rating, including cut sheets, of all piping and 
tubing to be used. 
5. A piping and instrumentation diagram that 
shows piping support and remote fill 
connections. 
6. Details of container venting, including but 
not limited to vent line size, material and 
termination location. 
7. Alarm and detection system and equipment, 
if applicable. 
8. Seismic support for containers. 
❖ 
This section provides a specific list of 
information about the system that must be 
documented and submitted in order for a 
permit to be issued. This list was developed 
based on a program created by Clark County, 
Nevada officials in dealing with this hazard. 
This list allows a proper review for compliance 
of each system and components. The list is 
necessary as these are unique systems with 
features different from those of typical 
hazardous material processes. 
5307.4.2 
Equipment. Pressure relief, vent 
piping, fill indicators, fill connections, vent 
terminations, piping systems and the storage, use 
and handling of the carbon dioxide shall be in 
accordance with Chapter 53 and NFPA 55. 
❖ 
This section simply requires compliance with 
NFPA 55 in addition to Chapter 53 for the 
system and all its components. Section 5301.1 
already requires compliance with NFPA 55, but 
due to the unique nature of these systems, the 
provisions are set up to provide a reminder for 
this requirement. Generally, Chapter 53 would 
apply to these systems unless something more 
specific is required in Section 5307.4. 
5307.4.3 
Gas detection system. A gas 
detection system complying with Section 916 shall 
be provided in rooms or indoor areas in which the 
carbon dioxide enrichment process is located, in 
rooms or indoor areas in which container systems 
are located, and in other areas where carbon dioxide 
is expected to accumulate. Carbon dioxide sensors 
shall be provided within 300 mm of the floor in the 
area where the gas is expected to accumulate or 
leaks are most likely to occur. The system shall be 
designed as follows: 
1. Activates a low-level alarm upon detection of 
a carbon dioxide concentration of 9000 
mg/m3. 
2. Activates a high-level alarm upon detection 
of a carbon dioxide concentration of 54 000 
mg/m3. 
❖ 
These provisions are modeled after the 
requirements in Section 5307.3, given that the 
hazards of CO2 are the same. The primary 
difference with these requirements is that 
Section 5307.3 allows gas detection as an 
alternative to ventilation whereas Section 
5307.4 requires both gas detection and 
ventilation. The thresholds for activation of the 
gas detection system are the same as those 
required by Section 5307.3. However, the 
activation of the gas detection system requires 
more actions to occur than simply providing 
alarm signals. These actions are addressed in 
Section 5307.4.3.1
