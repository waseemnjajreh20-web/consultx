# Section 6301.1

## Canonical Code Text

6301.1 Scope. The storage and use of oxidizing 
materials shall be in accordance with this chapter 
and Chapter 50. Oxidizing gases shall also comply 
with Chapter 53. Oxidizing cryogenic fluids shall 
also comply with Chapter 55. 
Exceptions: 
1. Display and storage in Group M and storage 
in Group S occupancies complying with 
Section 5003.11. 
2. Bulk oxygen systems at industrial and 
institutional consumer sites shall be in 
accordance with NFPA 55. 
3. Liquid oxygen stored or used in home health 
care in Group I-1, I-4 and R occupancies in 
accordance with section 6306. 
❖ 
This chapter is based in part on former NFPA
