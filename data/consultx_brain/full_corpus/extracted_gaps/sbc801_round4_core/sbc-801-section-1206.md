1206.1 General. Stationary fuel cell power 
systems in new and existing occupancies shall 
comply with this section. 
Exception: The temporary use of a fuel cell-
powered electric vehicle to power a Group R-3 or 
R-4 building while parked shall comply with 
Section 1206.14.
