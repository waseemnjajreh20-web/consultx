503.2.3 Surface. Fire apparatus access roads shall 
be designed and maintained to support the imposed 
loads of fire apparatus and shall be surfaced so as to 
provide all-weather driving capabilities. 
❖ 
This provision does not specify a particular type 
of surface. It is written in performance 
language; therefore, the surface must carry the 
load of the anticipated emergency response 
vehicles and be drivable in all kinds of weather. 
 
The term “all-weather driving capabilities” 
would typically require some type of paved or 
hard surface. Gravel would be prone to 
problems in areas subject to heavy rain where 
plowing could reduce the gravel roadbed to 
mud very quickly. Alternatives to concrete or 
asphalt, such as interlocking pavers, may be 
used when approved by the Fire official. 
Jurisdictions may benefit from developing a 
local 
policy 
outlining 
specific 
design 
requirements for fire apparatus access roads to 
clarify local interpretations of the section. The 
policy should include local requirements for 
surfacing and include acceptable surfacing 
materials.
