507.5 Fire hydrant systems. Fire hydrant 
systems shall comply with Sections 507.5.1 through 
507.5.6. 
❖ 
Where fire hydrant systems are part of the 
approved water supply, the system must 
comply with this section. 
507.5.1 Where required. Where a portion of the 
facility or building hereafter constructed or moved 
into or within the jurisdiction is more than 120 m 
from a hydrant on a fire apparatus access road, as 
measured by an approved route around the exterior 
of the facility or building, on-site fire hydrants and 
mains shall be provided where required by the Fire 
official. 
Exceptions: 
1. For Group R-3 and Group U occupancies, the 
distance requirement shall be 180 m. 
2. For buildings equipped throughout with an 
approved 
automatic 
sprinkler 
system 
installed 
in 
accordance 
with 
Section 
903.3.1.1or 
903.3.1.2, 
the 
distance 
requirement shall be 180 m. 
 
The intent is that not more than 120 m of hose 
will have to be laid out to reach all portions of 
the exterior grade level of the building. Each 
hydrant must be accessible to fire apparatus 
and the 120 m distance should be measured 
from the hydrant(s) to all portions of the exterior 
at ground level [see Commentary Figure 
507.5.1(1)]. When on-site hydrants and mains 
are required to achieve compliance with the 
distance requirement, this section gives the 
Fire official the authority to determine and 
approve hydrant locations on the site. 
 
This paragraph is not intended to prevent 
development in rural areas where fire hydrants 
are not available, as long as the Fire official has 
approved an alternative water supply. The 
alternative water supply could be a fire 
department  water tanker or a static, all-
weather water supply that is approved by the 
Fire official. 
 
In recognition of the smaller relative size and 
fire hazard characteristics of one-and two-
family dwellings and utility buildings, Exception 
1 increases the 120 m distance to 180 m [see 
Commentary Figure 507.5.1(2)]. Note that the 
one-and two-family dwellings classified in 
Group R-3 are those that are not within the 
scope of SBC 1101/1102, see section 102.5.
